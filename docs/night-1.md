# Night 1

What the first evening with Chronicle looks like, start to finish. Budget about an hour, once.

## Before you start

- Open the app on the device you will log from most nights. On iPhone, open it in Safari and use
  *Share → Add to Home Screen* so it runs full screen and keeps its storage. On Android, Chrome offers *Install*.
- Have Claude open in another tab or the Claude app. Haiku is enough for nightly letters; use Sonnet for the
  origin prompt and the weekly review.

## 1. The Origin Sheet (about 35 minutes)

Ten parts, 118 questions: Identity, Body, Skin, Mind, Dopamine, Work, Money, People, Goals, Today. Answer with
numbers where numbers exist. Skip anything you do not want to answer; you can come back from the Vault later.
Your answers save as you type, so you can close the app and resume.

If you would rather not send money details to Claude, turn on *Exclude money* in the Vault settings; the
Money part is kept on your device and left out of every prompt.

## 2. The origin prompt (5 minutes)

At the end of the sheet the app compiles it into one document and shows *Copy the prompt*. Paste it into a
new Claude chat (Sonnet). Claude replies with your State of Me, a first set of skills with levels, routines
with upsides and costs, goals, and your first morning letter. Paste the whole reply back.

You then review the suggestions: untick what you do not want, rename what reads wrong, and *Create my game*.

## 3. Tonight: the Campfire (20–30 minutes)

- Tick the routines you did. Streaks are counted from the logs; each routine has one shield a week that covers
  a single miss.
- Tap the numbers to set weight, sleep, mood, screen time, energy, urges resisted.
- Add meals from the chips or type a new one.
- One line about how today felt. One lesson you used, from a book you are reading. Three rotating questions.
- *Send tonight's log* closes the night, applies the routine scores to your pillars and copies the nightly prompt.
  Paste it into Claude (a new chat is fine; the prompt carries its own memory). Paste the reply back.

If Claude's reply is missing the `## BRIEF` section the app keeps it as a draft and tells you what is missing.
Ask Claude to answer in the format again and paste the new reply.

## 4. Tomorrow morning

The Morning screen shows the letter with a drop cap, your pillars with what changed overnight, three quests
with their real-life rewards, an affirmation you can keep, and any warnings (a streak at risk, a pattern).
Tick quests as you complete them. Tonight's Campfire will see them.

## Weekly and monthly

On Sundays, the Campfire offers a weekly review (Sonnet): a digest of the week and a rewritten State of Me.
Monthly digests compress further. This is what keeps the nightly prompt small as the months pass.

## Backups

The Vault exports a JSON backup of everything and a JSONL dataset of every prompt and reply. Export a backup
once a week, and always before switching devices. Browsers can clear site data for sites you have not opened
in a while; the Home Screen install and regular backups are the protection.
